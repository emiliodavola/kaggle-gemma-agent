## J-20261102-04 — Proposed storing embed_tokens_per_layer as int8 to lift the training window from 8192 to 12288 tokens

- **Time:** 12:18Z · **Actor:** opencode · **Type:** decision · **Status:** open
- **Why:** The table is 5.25 GiB of the 8.83 GiB resident after the downcast. At 12288 tokens the step peaks at 24.72 GiB on a 24 GiB card and spills; int8 recovers 2.63 GiB and brings it to about 22.1 GiB.
- **Evidence:** docs/proposals/20261102-int8-embedding-table.md; training/smoke_qlora.json
- **Refs:** docs/proposals/20261102-int8-embedding-table.md, docs/README.md

## J-20261102-05 — Stored embed_tokens_per_layer as int8, freeing 2.62 GiB and moving the training window from 8192 to 10240 tokens

- **Time:** 21:08Z · **Actor:** opencode · **Type:** change · **Status:** applied
- **Why:** The table held 5.25 GiB of the 8.83 GiB resident. Int8 with one scale per row, dequantized after the gather, costs 0.23% on the loss and takes a 10240-token step to 19.66 GB with a 12.6 s backward. 12288 allocates 22.29 GB and collapses to an 822 s backward on paging.
- **Evidence:** training/smoke_qlora.py; docs/proposals/20261102-int8-embedding-table.md
- **Refs:** PR #149, docs/proposals/20261102-int8-embedding-table.md, docs/proposals/20261101-lora-sft.md, training/README.md
